animated-lossy-offsets.webp is an animated (VP8X, ANIM and ANMF chunks) WebP
image with a 38×21 canvas and four lossy frames, some with an alpha channel
(an ALPH chunk) and some placed at a non-zero offset within the canvas. It was
made by libwebp 1.5.0's webpmux tool, run from the test/data directory:

webpmux \
  -frame hippopotamus.masked-with-muybridge.36x8.lossy.webp +100+0+0+0-b \
  -frame hippopotamus.masked-with-muybridge.36x8.lossy.webp +100+2+12+0-b \
  -frame peacock.24x19.lossy.webp +100+0+0+0-b \
  -frame peacock.24x19.lossy.webp +100+14+2+0-b \
  -loop 0 -bgcolor 255,0,0,0 -o artificial-webp/animated-lossy-offsets.webp

Each frame's duration is 100ms, its blending method is "do not blend" and its
disposal method is "do not dispose". The frames at (2, 12) and (14, 2) reach
the canvas' bottom and right edges.

Decoding frame 1 (or 3) should give the same pixels as decoding frame 0 (or 2),
other than being offset within the canvas.
